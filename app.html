🎯 Exam Strategy

Vocab/Grammar (30 min)

Focus on pattern recognition, not translation. Eliminate obviously wrong answers first. Read ALL options before choosing.

Reading (70 min)

Read the QUESTIONS before the passage. Skim for keywords. Don't get stuck on unknown words — context gives meaning.

Listening (40 min)

Catch the question word first (何, どこ, いつ, だれ, どうして). Netflix immersion trains this naturally.

Passing Score: 95/180 total, with minimum score in EACH section. You can't ace vocab and bomb listening.

💡 The Prodigy Method

Read once. Internalize. Then just follow the dashboard daily.

🔥 Rule #1: The 5-Minute Minimum (MOST IMPORTANT)

▼

On bad days, tired days, busy days: study for exactly 5 minutes.

  • Review 5 items from the Weakness Bank
  • Write 3 kanji from memory
  • Watch 5 minutes of a Japanese show
This counts. Your streak survives. Never zero days.
The habit of showing up matters more than the length of the session. A 5-minute day beats a 0-minute day by infinity percent.

🧠 Rule #2: Active Recall Over Passive Review

▼

WRONG Ways to Study

  • Re-reading vocabulary lists
  • Copying kanji while looking at them
  • Watching videos without pausing to test yourself

RIGHT Ways to Study

  • Cover the answer, try to produce it from memory
  • Look at kanji, close eyes, write from memory, THEN check
  • Pause Netflix, try to predict what they'll say next
The struggle IS the learning. If it feels easy, you're not learning. The pain of trying to recall is your brain building neural pathways.

🎯 Rule #3: Weakness-First Attack

▼
  • Every session starts with your weakest items (Weakness Bank SRS reviews)
  • The thing you avoid studying is the thing you most need to study
  • New material only AFTER reviews are done
Your Weakness Bank items with 0/5 streak are your #1 priority. Items you keep getting wrong need different approaches — add mnemonics, find them in Netflix shows, use them in real life.

🔁 Rule #4: SRS Without Apps

▼

Spaced Repetition intervals built into this system:

  • Got it right at review: 1 day → 3 days → 7 days → 14 days → 30 days
  • Got it wrong: Go back one interval level
  • 5 correct recalls across 30 days = MASTERED (graduates to Never Forget)
The SRS Review page handles all this automatically. Just show up and review what's due. The system does the scheduling.

🎥 Rule #5: Sentence Mining from Netflix & Life

▼
  • Don't learn words in isolation
  • Learn them inside sentences you actually heard
  • 届く alone is forgettable
  • 荷物が届いた from a show you watched is memorable
When adding to the Weakness Bank, always include the sentence or context where you encountered the word. Your brain anchors new words to experiences, not dictionary definitions.

🔀 Rule #6: Interleaving (Mix It Up)

▼
  • Don't do 1 hour of just vocabulary
  • Mix: 15 min vocab → 10 min grammar → 10 min kanji → 15 min listening
  • Your brain forms stronger connections when switching between types
This feels harder than blocked practice, but research shows it produces 2-3x better long-term retention. The difficulty is the signal that it's working.

🇯🇵 Rule #7: Daily Life = Study Time

▼

You live in Japan. Everything is practice:

  • Read signs, menus, receipts (kanji practice)
  • Listen to train announcements (listening)
  • Speak in Japanese at konbini and restaurants
  • Notice words you don't know and add to Weakness Bank
The entire country is your classroom. A 30-minute trip to the konbini can teach more than an hour of textbook study if you're paying attention.

🚫 The Anti-Restart Protocol

▼

When you come back after a gap (and you will):

  1. Open My Level page (see exactly where you are)
  2. Open SRS Review (see what needs review)
  3. Do ONE review session (even 5 minutes)
  4. You're back. Streak resets to 1. Build from there.
You NEVER go back to zero. Your mastered items are proven mastered. Your Weakness Bank knows your gaps. The system remembers even when you forget. There is no "starting over" — only "picking up where you left off."
